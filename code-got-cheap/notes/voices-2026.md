## Possible "two views" pairings

1. Review: Orosz ("no engineer can keep up with 5-10 times more code") + Ronacher ("I was the bottleneck all along") vs Yegge's "never looked at the code" approach; Willison finds the blur "quite upsetting".
2. End of engineering vs golden age: Amodei (6–12 months) vs Booch ("your problems are not [changing]") vs Marcus.
3. Jobs: Huang ("complete nonsense") vs Hightower ("not hiring juniors as much").
4. Metrics: Majors defends mandates vs Tacho/METR (gains flat or unmeasurable).
5. Interviews: Yegge (technical interviews ending) vs companies screening for "AI positivity".
6. Framing slide: Majors, "The enthusiasts are not wrong." / "The skeptics are also not wrong."
