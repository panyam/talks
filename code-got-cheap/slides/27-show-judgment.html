This is the most practical slide for job seekers. Slow down here. Seven clicks: one per line of the transcript, then the interviewer's note.

Read the candidate lines in a normal voice and the AI line in an over-eager one. left-pad-ultra is made up, the original left-pad is the npm package whose removal broke a lot of builds in 2016, so some people will get the joke.

The five moves: say what you're asking and why (your prompt is part of your answer), read the output critically and out loud, reject something and explain it, test before they ask, and know when the tool isn't worth it.

What the interviewer is really trying to work out is whether they'd trust you to merge code into their repo. That's the note at the bottom.
