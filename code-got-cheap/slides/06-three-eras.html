Three eras in three years

Autocomplete

Finishes your line.

An eager intern who's read every repo on Earth.

Chat

Writes the function you describe.

The intern now talks back. Confidently.

Agents

Takes a ticket, edits many files, runs tests, opens the PR.

The intern has commit access and doesn't sleep.

Keep this light and quick. Most of the audience lived through all three.

Point: each step moved the human further from typing and closer to directing and checking. That's the thread for the rest of the talk.
