Be very clear out loud: these numbers are invented. It's a cartoon of a dashboard and doesn't come from any real company.

The top row is what's easy to measure. The bottom row is what we tend not to measure yet. Keep it light, the joke is on dashboards in general.

Then click for the real data, which is the good news: Faros AI Q3 2026 (22,000 devs, 4,000 teams) found incidents per PR up 14.5% under high AI adoption, compared with 242.7% in their previous report (Apr 2026). Both are increases, so incidents per PR are still higher with heavy AI use, but the extra amount has shrunk from about 3.4× to about 1.15×. Faros doesn't say whether the two reports used the same sample, so call it a trend across their reports, not a controlled comparison. Teams are adapting. Vendor data, say so if asked.

Same report: "Each change is safer, and there are far more of them." Monthly incidents are up 125.4%, PRs merged with no review at all are up 76.3%, time in QA is up 300.6%. So the win is per change, and the open problem is volume, which is the review capacity point again.

Why it improved: Faros doesn't say. My interpretation, label it as that: newer models make fewer basic mistakes than early-2026 ones, teams have added guardrails (CI checks, AI-use policies like Ghostty's), and AI-assisted review is spreading (JetBrains 2026: 43% of developers are ready to fully delegate code review, up from 33%). It's probably all three.
