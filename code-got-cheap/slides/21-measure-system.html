What to measure instead

Flow

How long from idea to production? Where does work wait?

Quality

How often do changes break things? How much gets rewritten?

People

Are reviewers drowning? Are juniors learning? Ask them.

A useful sanity check for any productivity number: did customers notice?

The pictures are there for a smile, don't explain them. The snail's PR has been "in review since Tues", and the bug is hiding under the LGTM stamp.

DORA's four keys still work: deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, time to restore. Add rework and review wait time for the AI era.

For individual contributors: if your company tracks "% AI usage", these are the outcome numbers to put next to it. Managers usually just want a number they can defend.
