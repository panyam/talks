The junior pipeline

If AI does the junior work, where do the seniors come from?
  • Senior engineers are people who did a lot of junior work and remember the scars.
  • Cutting junior hiring saves money now and leaves fewer senior engineers in five years.
  • Teams that keep hiring juniors and pair them with AI and people are betting on having seniors later. I think that's a good bet.

Play this one straight. No jokes. It's the most important slide in the teams section.
